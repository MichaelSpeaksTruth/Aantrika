# AANTRIKA Design System

## Visual Identity
- Brand direction: Editorial eco-luxury with modern conscious feminism.
- Primary mood: soft organic brutalism.
- Feel: raw but refined, organic but technological, bold but calming.
- Avoid typical feminine wellness clichés and stay premium, tactile, and deeply human.

## Color Palette
- Cotton White: `#F7F3EE`
- Warm Sand: `#E8DED1`
- Moss Green: `#5F6F52`
- Sage: `#A9B29D`
- Terracotta: `#C97C5D`
- Dusty Rose: `#D9B8A8`
- Deep Soil Brown: `#3E322C`
- Charcoal Ink: `#1F1F1F`

## Typography
- Headings: large editorial sans-serif with strong letterforms and cinematic scale.
- Accent text: elegant serif for emotional storytelling.
- Body: clean, warm, highly readable text with generous leading.
- Suggested font pairing:
  - Sans: Inter
  - Serif: Cormorant Garamond

## Motion & Interactions
- Entry motion: slow fade reveals with gentle upward movement.
- Hover states: subtle elevation, soft scale, thin highlight border.
- Background motion: floating organic shapes and slow parallax layers.
- Transition easing: `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` for premium softness.

## Layout Principles
- Broken grid compositions with large whitespace and offset alignments.
- Layered cards and tactile surfaces that feel like paper and cloth.
- Uneven rhythm: alternating margins, asymmetric paddings, and floating text blocks.
- Organic dividers: curved separators, soft borders, and layered micro-texture.

## Component System
### Buttons
- Matte surface, soft corners, thin earthy border.
- Raised hover states with slight shadow and translation.
- Use bold uppercase or strong sentence case for CTAs.
- Primary button: `soil` background, `cotton` text.
- Secondary button: transparent/outlined with `soil` text.

### Cards
- Paper-inspired surfaces with soft shadows.
- Layered depth using inset lines and offset floating shapes.
- Use organic clipping and uneven text alignment for editorial energy.

### Sections
- Hero: large cinematic headline + layered highlight cards.
- About: founder story with mission/vision and core value badges.
- Product: tactile materials breakdown with feature cards.
- Sustainability: metrics and impact storytelling with organic imagery.
- Community: workshop origin story and warm human context.

## Spacing
- Avoid perfect symmetry and tight horizontal bands.
- Use large vertical spacing between sections.
- Alternate content alignment left/right to create motion.
- Provide generous padding inside cards: `2.5rem`–`3rem`.

## Tailwind Theme Extensions
- Custom colors: `cotton`, `sand`, `moss`, `sage`, `terracotta`, `dustyRose`, `soil`, `ink`.
- Shadows: `soft`, `paper`, `inward`.
- Border radius: `soft`, `panel`.
- Animations: `float`, `reveal`.

## Accessibility
- Use high contrast in text over backgrounds.
- Keep body text at a minimum of 1rem with `line-height: 1.7`.
- Provide semantic structure for screen readers.
- Ensure CTA buttons are distinct and keyboard focus visible.

## Production Notes
- Keep imagery editorial and tactile through layered shapes rather than flat stock.
- Prefer locally hosted SVGs and minimal iconography for a premium feel.
- Use responsive-first typography and preserve rhythm across breakpoints.
